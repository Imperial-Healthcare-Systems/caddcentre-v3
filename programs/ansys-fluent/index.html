Curriculum

What you will learn

The sequence follows how the work is actually done, not how the software's menus are organised.

  • Governing principles without the maths overload
  • Where CFD is reliable and where it is not
  • Domain definition
  • Mesh strategy for flow
  • Boundary layer resolution
  • Mesh quality checks
  • Boundary conditions
  • Turbulence model selection
  • Material and operating conditions
  • Solver settings
  • Convergence monitoring
  • Diagnosing divergence
  • Contours, vectors and streamlines
  • Quantitative extraction
  • Result validation

Where this leads

CFD is a specialist route with fewer practitioners and correspondingly less competition. It suits engineers who are comfortable with uncertainty and willing to defend a result rather than just produce a colourful picture.

Certification

Three levels. Pick the one that matches your goal.

Ansys Fluent is certified at three levels. Most people start at Proficient; which level actually suits you depends on your background and how far you want to go — we will tell you straight at counselling.

Proficient

Core design and drafting skills. The working competence an employer expects on day one.

Masters

Advanced modelling and project workflows. Depth beyond the basics, on real project structures.

Expert

Complete project management and multidisciplinary collaboration skills.

Hours and fees vary by level. Book a counselling session and we will confirm exactly which level suits your background.

Eligibility

Who this is for

  • Students pursuing or completed Mechanical, Automotive or Aeronautical Engineering
  • Diploma / ITI students in mechanical or production disciplines
  • Production and quality engineers moving into design
  • Professionals in manufacturing, product development or tooling

No prior software experience is assumed at Proficient level.
