Build a career in
Architecture & Visualisation.

Visualisation is persuasion. The model has to be right, but the image has to make somebody feel something about a building that does not exist yet.

Architectural Designer · 3D Visualiser · Design Technician · Interior Visualiser

Talk to a career advisor Not sure? Try the finder
The work

What this work actually involves

This path builds both halves — accurate modelling, and the lighting, material and rendering craft that turns it into work a practice can present to a client. You will learn where realism matters and where it is wasted effort.

It suits people who already have design sense and want the technical craft to express it.

Skills roadmap

The sequence that gets you there

SketchUp / Revit→Materials & lighting→V-Ray→Lumion→3ds Max→Portfolio project
