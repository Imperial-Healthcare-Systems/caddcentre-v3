Build a career in
Structural Engineering & Analysis.

This path is for engineers who want the calculation as well as the geometry. You will model structures, apply loads, run analysis, interpret results against codes, and produce the detailing that a site can actually build from.

Structural Design Engineer · Analysis Engineer · Detailing Engineer · Structural BIM Engineer

Talk to a career advisor Not sure? Try the finder
The work

What this work actually involves

Analysis without detailing is academic; detailing without analysis is guesswork. The roles that pay well need both, which is why this path keeps them together.

It suits people who are comfortable being the person who has to justify a number.

Skills roadmap

The sequence that gets you there

AutoCAD→Structural modelling→STAAD.Pro→ETABS→Load cases & code checks→Revit Structure detailing

  • Someone targeting consultancy rather than site work

Probably not the right start if

  • You prefer coordination and information management — BIM is the better path
  • You want product rather than building structures — see Mechanical & Product Design
