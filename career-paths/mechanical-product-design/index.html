Build a career in
Mechanical & Product Design.

Product design is where a drawing has to survive contact with a machine shop. A model that looks right and cannot be made is a failed design.

Design Engineer · Product Design Engineer · CAD Engineer · Detailing Engineer

Talk to a career advisor Not sure? Try the finder
The work

What this work actually involves

This path teaches parametric modelling, assemblies, and the tolerancing language that makes a design manufacturable — then puts a printed prototype of your own part in your hand so you find out what your design got wrong.

The manufacturing belt around Gurugram, Manesar and Bhiwadi runs on exactly this skill set.

Skills roadmap

The sequence that gets you there

AutoCAD Mechanical→SolidWorks→Assemblies→GD&T→CATIA / NX CAD→3D printed prototype
